Supplement & Food Guide

General education for informed fitness choices.

🥛PROTEIN

Whey / Plant Protein

A convenient protein source when food alone does not meet an individual's needs. Regular foods such as eggs, milk, curd, dal, paneer, fish and chicken are also options.

Check ingredients, allergens and product quality. Supplements are optional, not compulsory.
🏋️CREATINE

Creatine Monohydrate

A researched sports supplement used by some adults for strength and high-intensity training. It is not suitable for everyone.

Seek medical advice first if you have kidney disease, take regular medicines, are pregnant or have other health concerns.
🍊MICRONUTRIENTS

Vitamins & Minerals

Prioritise a varied diet. Use vitamin or mineral supplements only when appropriate for your needs.

Avoid high-dose or multiple supplements without professional guidance.

Everyday Food Ideas

Simple, familiar options — adjust portions to individual needs.

🥚 Protein foods

Eggs, milk, curd, paneer, dal, chickpeas, fish, chicken, tofu

🍚 Energy foods

Rice, idli, dosa, oats, millet, whole-wheat chapati, fruits

🥬 Vegetables & healthy fats

Greens, seasonal vegetables, nuts, seeds and suitable cooking oils

⚠

CLIENT OWN RISK & DISCLAIMER

Please read before participating in training or using supplements.

  • Fitness activities involve physical effort and some risk of injury. Clients should work within their own ability and stop if they feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness.
  • Clients must disclose relevant medical conditions, injuries, allergies, medicines and other health concerns to the trainer before starting.
  • Food and supplement information on this website is general education only, not medical advice, diagnosis or a personalised diet prescription.
  • Clients choose whether to use any supplement. Check labels, ingredients, expiry dates and possible interactions; consult a qualified healthcare professional before use when unsure.
  • The gym and trainer do not guarantee specific results. Results vary by individual. Clients are responsible for making informed decisions and following professional medical advice.

This notice is a general safety statement and does not replace a properly drafted waiver, informed-consent process or local legal advice.
